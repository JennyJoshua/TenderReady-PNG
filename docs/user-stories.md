# User stories
- As a carpenter, I save my business details once so I do not retype them on every quotation.
- As a caterer, I make a quotation with items, a delivery charge and a discount, and send it as a PDF on WhatsApp.
- As a cleaner, I choose whether tax applies and what rate to show, because I know my own situation.
- As a security provider, I write a capability statement from my profile and edit the wording before sending.
- As a supplier, I add each thing a tender asks for and tick them off, and see how many are left.
- As a transport operator, I keep a general readiness list and mark items not applicable.
- As any owner, I reopen an old quotation, change it, and export it again without creating a duplicate.
- As any owner, I work with no signal, and I can delete everything from my phone when I want.
- As any owner, I am never told the app has checked my compliance or guaranteed a contract.
