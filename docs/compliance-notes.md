# Compliance notes

This is not legal advice. The app makes no claim about legal, tax or procurement compliance.

- No registration, tax, licence or eligibility check is done. Registration and TIN status are only what the user selects, and are not copied into documents automatically.
- Tax is off by default; the user decides whether it applies and enters name and rate. The app never assumes a rate.
- Checklists are personal planning aids, not official assessments. Requirements vary by business, industry, jurisdiction and opportunity.
- Capability statements say they are based on information provided by the business owner and are unverified.
- The app does not generate qualifications, references, projects, certifications or approvals.
- Privacy: data stays on the device, in unencrypted app-private storage. No accounts, analytics, ads or servers. Exported PDFs are ordinary files under the user's control; deleting app data does not remove them or copies already sent.
- If the app is ever published or used commercially, check PNG privacy, consumer and tax rules and get proper advice.
